# Global Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Global Super Store sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance through an interactive dashboard.

---

## Objective

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track profit across regions
- Compare product categories
- Monitor shipping performance
- Build an interactive business dashboard

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
- Dashboard Design

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Regions
- Sales
- Profit
- Shipping

## Dashboard Features

### KPIs

- Total Profit

### Visualizations

- Sales and Profit by Sub-Category
- Profit Margin(%) by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Total sales and profit by customer segment
- Top 5 Products by sales
- Average delivery days of ship mode
- Sales and Volume by Order priority
- Regional Profit Margin(%)

### Filters

- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Sub-category
- Order Priorty
- Ship mode
- Market

## Key Insights


-💰 Technology category generates the highest sales and profit, mainly driven by Phones and Accessories.
-🪑 Furniture category has low profit margins due to heavy discounts and high shipping costs.
-🌍 The West Region shows the strongest profit margin, while Central lags behind in profitability.
-👥 Consumer Segment contributes the highest number of orders, while Corporate Segment yields better profit per order.
-📦 Top 5 products contribute a large share of total revenue — showing dependency on a few high-performing products.
-📅 Sales peak during Q4 (Oct–Dec), indicating strong holiday season demand.
🕒 Average delivery time is around 3.9 days, reflecting efficient logistics and order management.
